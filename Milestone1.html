Many different materials go into the manufacturing of CPUs.
Some of which include:

  • Copper:
    Copper is mostly mined using open pit mining methods. In open pit mining a series of stepped benches are dug sequentially deeper into the earth. The ore is removed by inserting explosives into holes drilled by boring machines.
  • Silicon:
    Silicon is obtained mainly from quartz which is mosrt likely the second most common material on earth. Quartz is aquired mostly just from scooping up sand.
  • Iron:
    Iron is extracted by pit mining in which they dig progressivly deeper steps extracting ore as they go. The ore is extracted by grinding the rock into pebble sized pieces and the iron is separated by using a large electromagnet
  • Gold:
    Gold is extracted using hard rock mining which means it is extracted from the rock in which it was originally deposited
  • Aluminum:
    Aluminum is produced by mining bauxite and processing it to produce aluminum
  • Nickel:
    Nickel is mined using strip mining to extract the deeper deposits and a variety of methods are used to estract it from the material aroound it.
