Computer-aided design (CAD) software is used to create, modify, and analyze 2D and 3D designs and models in a digital environment.
CAD software is used by many professionals, including architects, engineers, designers, and artists. CAD (Computer-Aided Design) software is used to create 2d and 3d virtual images of products, properties, and other things you might need to visualize before actually creating. Especially with complex models such as a GPU the designs can become hard to imagine so CAD software is essential to planning. CAD can even account for different materials properties and uses. Electrical components are also able to be drawn in CAD. Which aids the design of other electrical components that go into GPUs.
